## Source and outcome

- Source specification: `specs/001-mvp-document-intelligence.md`, sections 3, F3, F4, 9 and 10.
- User outcome: the interface visibly communicates initial loading, active work, synchronization and AI generation; answers keep source links in the separate citations area and show shorter excerpts.
